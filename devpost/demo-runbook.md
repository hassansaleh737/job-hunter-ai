# Job Hunter AI — Two-Minute Demo Runbook

This is a recording shot plan, not a narration script. The entrant should describe the project in their own words while following the visible sequence below.

## Recording Preparation

- Use a synthetic, redacted, or explicitly demo-safe text-based PDF CV. Do not record a real private CV.
- Close `.env`, terminals containing environment values, email, notifications, and unrelated browser tabs.
- Prepare either a verified Live Search run or a dated snapshot with its collection date visible.
- Confirm the selected results contain one strong skills match with an explicit eligibility conflict and one `Unknown` eligibility result.
- Keep the recording in English and between one minute and three minutes; target approximately two minutes.
- Do not use copyrighted music, third-party trademarks, or other material without permission.

## Shot Plan

### 0:00–0:12 — Problem and Product

Show the upload screen and the five-step indicator.

Entrant narration prompts:

- Who experiences the job-search problem?
- What wastes their time today?
- What does evidence-based matching change?

### 0:12–0:30 — Upload CV

Show the accepted text-based PDF format, session-only privacy notice, provider disclosure, consent control, and the single **Analyze CV** action. Upload the prepared demo CV.

Visible proof:

- The CV is accepted.
- The app advances to profile review.
- No token, local path, private document content, or provider console is shown.

### 0:30–0:48 — Review Profile

Briefly show Skills, Education, Experience, and Relevant Projects. Correct one harmless demo value or point out the extracted-versus-user-provided distinction, then confirm the profile.

Visible proof:

- The user controls the profile used for matching.
- Missing information is not invented.

### 0:48–1:02 — Preferences

Select one or more target roles, the experience level, Egypt as the applying-from country, and any employment preferences used for the prepared results. Keep **Remote Only** visible.

Visible proof:

- Required preferences are explicit.
- Paid-only or employment-type uncertainty is not silently guessed.

### 1:02–1:22 — Agent Activity

Start Live Search or explicitly choose the prepared Dated Snapshot. Show the actual stage labels for source search, verification, matching, and result preparation.

Visible proof:

- Remotive and Greenhouse are named as tools or sources where applicable.
- Dated Snapshot mode displays its date and non-live label.
- Deterministic checks are not presented as hidden AI reasoning.

### 1:22–1:50 — Evidence-Based Results

Open a result with `Strong` skills match and `Not Eligible` eligibility because of an explicit restriction. Then open a result with `Unknown` eligibility because critical evidence is missing.

Visible proof:

- Skills match and eligibility are separate.
- The restriction or unresolved condition is readable.
- Source attribution, freshness information, evidence excerpts, and the original job link are visible.

### 1:50–2:00 — Closing Proof

Return to the results summary and leave the defining behavior visible: a strong skills match does not mean eligible.

Entrant narration prompts:

- What is the distinctive product decision?
- What did the working proof of concept demonstrate?

## Recording Acceptance Check

- [ ] The application is visibly functioning end to end, not shown only through slides or source code.
- [ ] The recording is under three minutes.
- [ ] No credentials, private CV content, personal notifications, or unrelated windows are visible.
- [ ] Live and snapshot results are labeled honestly.
- [ ] The video shows the problem, intended user, core workflow, and evidence-based distinction.
- [ ] The uploaded YouTube or Vimeo link is publicly viewable without signing in.
